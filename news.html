Traffic openings 2026

We are continuously looking for qualified, self-motivated graduate students and post-docs (Italian and international) with a background in networking, programming or statistical data analysis, in at least one of these areas:

  • Network and traffic measurement and analysis
    • Broadband monitoring and censorship detection (project partially funded by Google)
    • High-performance traffic generation and classification (with industry partners worldwide)
    • Active probing and network tracerouting
  • Cloud networking and SDN/NFV
  • Smart city and mobility (with industry partners worldwide)

Send an email with subject “Traffic Openings” to pescape AT unina.it, including your CV (with photo, research web page and at least two references), transcripts, publication list and other related credentials.
