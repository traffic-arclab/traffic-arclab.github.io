Citings and Links

We have a strong tradition of collaboration with both other Universities and Industries. To support network research and engineering of high performance research networks, our activities have created and continue to build networks analysis tools. D-ITG is one of the platforms we released.

We actively solicit collaborations with other network researchers, which increase the breadth and scope of our work and improve the understanding of performance issues and parameters in studying networks.

Many researchers use our tool for a variety of research and engineering purposes. The following is a listing of links where D-ITG is present. This page contains information on publications, researchers, and educators referencing our tool. For papers citings D-ITG please refer to papersciting.html.

If you are using D-ITG for scientific papers, academic lectures, project reports, or technical documents, please help us increasing D-ITG impact by adding a reference to:
