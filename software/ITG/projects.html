Research Projects

We have a strong tradition of collaboration with both other Universities and Industries in the field of national and international research projects. D-ITG versions have been released during several research projects:

  1. INTERMON -IST-2001-34123, Advanced architecture for INTER-domain quality of service MONitoring, modelling and visualisation:
    http://www.ist-intermon.org/

  2. WEBMINDS :: Wide-scalE, Broadband, MIddleware for Network Distributed Services:
    http://web-minds.consorzio-cini.it/

  3. E - NEXT :: Network of Excellence Emerging Network Technologies:
    http://www.ist-e-next.net/index.php

  4. CdC-ICT :: Centro di Competenza Regionale sulle Tecnologie dell’Informazione e della Comunicazione:
    http://www.crdc-ict.unisannio.it/

  5. QuaSAR: Quality and Controllability of Communication Services over Heterogeneous Networks. Funded by the Italian Ministry of Education, University and Research (MIUR) as part of the PRIN 2004 Funding Program:
    http://www.quasar-project.org/
