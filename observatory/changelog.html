Changelog

Every change to the catalogue, release by release: datasets added or removed, and the fields corrected in each record, with their old and new values.

Each release is a dated state of the catalogue that can be cited. Changes are collected under Unreleased until the next release, whose number depends on what changed:

  • Patch (e.g. 1.0.1): corrections to existing records only.
  • Minor (e.g. 1.1): datasets added to or removed from the catalogue.
  • Major (e.g. 2.0): a change of the taxonomy, such as a new axis, domain or vocabulary.

Each change shows whether it was made in the site editor or directly in the repository, and its commit. A dataset whose source stops distributing it stays in the catalogue, marked Withdrawn.

Every release is frozen as a copy of the catalogue at its date, and every dataset keeps its own dated history in its card: two works that cite the same dataset can be compared on the state of its record at the date or release each of them used.
