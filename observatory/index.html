About the Observatory

Many datasets are used to evaluate AI methods for intrusion detection, malware and command-and-control analysis, and anomaly detection, but the same dataset name often refers to different releases, captures and derived feature tables.

The Observatory records every dataset at the level of the release and its distributed artifacts, and links it to its original publication and distribution source.

Units of analysis

  • Family A named line of related collections (e.g. the Stratosphere Malware Capture Facility).
  • Release A documented distribution at a given stage (e.g. IoT-23 v1).
  • Capture A single acquisition event or scenario inside a release.
  • Artifact A file or package actually delivered to researchers (full PCAP, flow-only archive, CSV).

Submit your dataset

If you have collected and released a network-traffic dataset for cybersecurity research, propose it for the Observatory: it will be listed with its paper and distribution source.

You can also point us to a dataset by others that is not in the catalogue, or report that the information about a listed dataset has changed. Every submission is reviewed against the eligibility criterion before it is added.

A dataset is included when it distributes recorded network communications (packet captures, flow records, wireless frames, CAN or V2X messages, protocol logs) or features demonstrably derived from them, whatever the security task. Host-only telemetry, malware binaries and sensor time series without a network-traffic component are not eligible on their own.
