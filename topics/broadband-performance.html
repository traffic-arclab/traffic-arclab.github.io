Broadband performance

Measuring broadband access performance from the user side, with the BISmark home-router platform and the HoBBIT host-based platform, including satellite broadband.

Description

How fast is a broadband connection really, and what limits it? The group measures broadband access performance from the user side, where the experience of customers can be observed directly.

  • BISmark (Broadband Internet Service Benchmark) is a platform for broadband Internet and home network research based on instrumented home routers, developed together with Georgia Tech.
  • HoBBIT (Host-Based Broadband Internet Telemetry) monitors broadband performance from the hosts of the user network.

The measurements also cover new generation satellite broadband Internet services, compared with ADSL and 3G.
