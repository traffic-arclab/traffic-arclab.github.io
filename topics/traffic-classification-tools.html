Traffic classification tools

Tools developed by the group for traffic classification and monitoring: TIE, Plab and the WWAN performance monitor for mobile broadband networks.

Tools & projects

  • TIE
  • Plab
  • WWAN

    We develop techniques and tools to uncover the performance of operational, wireless, wide area networks. In particular, we focus on 3/4G and satellite networks.
