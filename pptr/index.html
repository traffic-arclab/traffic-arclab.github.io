Experimenting with alternative path tracing solutions

Paper Abstract

Tracing Internet paths is essential for gathering knowledge about the complex, highly dynamic, and largely opaque ecosystem of networks the Internet is. Currently, only two practical solutions are available: (i) equipping packets with the Record Route IP option to register addresses of the traversed routers; or (ii) eliciting ICMP Time Exceeded messages by limiting the Time-to-Live of the injected packets. In this paper, we propose three novel path tracing solutions designed to elicit ICMP Parameter Problem messages from the network by injecting malformed packets. We first experimentally evaluate their ability to actually collect replies from the traversed routers. Then, thanks to a large-scale multi-vantage points measurement campaign, we evaluate the ability of the most promising ICMP Parameter Problem-based solution to discover interfaces and routers not reported by the Paris-Traceroute Multipath Detection Algorithm (MDA), the state-of-the-art path tracing solution. Experimental results confirm the ability of this alternative path tracing approach to report thousands of interfaces and routers that are not reported by MDA.
