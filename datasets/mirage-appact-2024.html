MIRAGE-AppAct-2024

Traffic of 20 popular mobile apps, labeled with both the app and the specific activity performed (chat, audio and video calls, gaming, streaming).

NewCC BY-NC-ND 4.0

MIRAGE-AppAct-2024 takes into consideration the traffic generated by more than 240 experimenters using 20 popular mobile apps via 3 devices.

Each app was used to perform at least one of the following activities: Chat (Chat), Audio-call (ACall), Online Gaming (Gaming), Video-call (VCall) and Video-streaming (Streaming).

The dataset is released in JSON format, making available the raw traffic data captured.

How to cite

If you use MIRAGE-AppAct-2024 for scientific papers, academic lectures, project reports or technical documents, please help us increase its impact by citing:

Idio Guarino, Domenico Ciuonzo, Antonio Montieri, Antonio Pescapè, “MIRAGE-APPxACT-2024: A Novel Dataset for Mobile App and Activity Traffic Analysis”, Under submission, 2024.

To cite the dataset itself:

Guarino, I., Ciuonzo, D., Montieri, A., & Pescapè, A. (2026). MIRAGE-AppAct-2024 [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23158977
